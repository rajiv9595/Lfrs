Key LFSR Concepts for Your Presentation:
An all-zero seed remains locked at 0000 for XOR-feedback LFSRs because $0 \oplus 0 = 0$. Not every tap pair produces a maximal-length sequence. For a 4-bit maximal sequence (m-sequence), the period is $2^4 - 1 = 15$ non-zero states (e.g., taps Q3 & Q0 or Q3 & Q2).
Polynomial: $x^4 + x + 1$ (Taps Q3, Q0)
PRBS Pattern: Pseudo-Random Binary Sequence
Applications: SerDes Testing (PRBS7/15/31), Cryptography, CRC, GPS Gold codes
